# Fictional Athlete Profile

> This profile is fictional. It demonstrates the stable context that a skill
> reads before interpreting a single session.

## Coaching context

| Topic | Fictional context |
| --- | --- |
| Primary goal | Build toward an example 10 km event with durable quality work. |
| Availability | Four running days, one optional strength session, one full rest day. |
| Current constraint | No reported pain; protect recovery after quality sessions. |
| Athlete preference | Understand the reason for a change before it enters the plan. |

## Heart-rate and pace context

Easy runs are judged first by conversational effort and heart-rate stability,
not by chasing a pace. Quality targets are ranges, not a pass/fail test. The
machine-readable zones and records belong in the local `athlete_config.py`.

## Coach Model

### Evidence-backed observations

- After two controlled quality sessions followed by an easy day, fictional
  recovery markers and athlete narrative remained stable.
- A short reduction in volume preserved quality execution better than adding a
  second hard session during a busy week.

### Candidates to revisit

- A hard strength session may make the next quality day feel less controlled.
  This remains a candidate until it appears in at least two comparable weeks.

### Safety rule

Pain, an explicit athlete limit, or a material recovery signal overrides the
planned target. The skill records the evidence before proposing any change.
