## Adapted plan

Not active — following the original plan as written. This section is where
you'd document any deviation (injury adjustment, pace correction, life-driven
cutback) and which version has priority.

### Weekly adaptation rules (traffic-light protocol)

- 🟢 **Green** (sessions completed, feeling normal+): next week stays as planned.
- 🟡 **Yellow** (one quality session cut, or feeling was bad): next quality
  session gets easier and volume trims ~20%.
- 🔴 **Red** (2+ bad sessions, easy-day HR elevated, or pain that alters
  stride): cutback week — easy only, no quality, re-evaluate.
