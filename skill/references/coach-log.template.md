# Coach Log — Athlete Feedback and Coaching Decisions

> **Purpose**: this is where whatever Garmin doesn't capture lives — the
> athlete's own words (how it felt, niggles, life context, why a session got
> skipped) and the coaching decisions made from that. FIT-file feeling/effort
> is a number; this is the *why*.
>
> **Always load during the weekly check-in and before adapting the plan.**
> Newest entries go at the TOP.
> Entry format: date, feedback (summarized, faithful to what was said), the
> coach's read, the decision made.
> Never delete old entries — they're the record of what worked and what didn't.
