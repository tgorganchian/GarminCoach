## Adapted plan (if different from the source plan)

If you're deviating from the original plan — corrected paces, an injury
adjustment, a life-driven cutback — document it here with the reasoning, and
note which one has priority. Keep the history of what changed and why; don't
just overwrite silently.

### Weekly adaptation rules (traffic-light protocol)

Define your own thresholds here. A reasonable starting point:

- 🟢 **Green** (sessions completed, feeling normal+): next week stays as planned.
- 🟡 **Yellow** (one quality session cut, or feeling was bad): dial back the
  next quality session and trim volume ~20%.
- 🔴 **Red** (2+ bad sessions, easy-day HR elevated, or pain that alters
  stride): cutback week — easy only, no quality, re-evaluate.

Run this check every time you review the week's feedback (Step 6 in
`SKILL.md`).
